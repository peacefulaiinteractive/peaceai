# 🕊️ PeaceAI

**PeaceAI** is an open-source, AI-powered global tool for fast, fair, and free conflict resolution.  
It is designed to help individuals, communities, and even governments de-escalate tension and reach peace within 10 minutes.

---

## 🌍 Mission

- Promote peace education and conflict resolution skills globally
- Build a free and accessible de-escalation assistant powered by ethical AI
- Eliminate the need for war, violence, and control through dialogue and empathy
- Crowdsource peacebuilding and invite contributions from every culture

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. **Input**: A conflict is submitted in text, audio, or video
2. **Analysis**: AI reads tone, context, and emotion
3. **Suggestions**: Multiple peaceful outcomes are generated
4. **Resolution**: A clear, calming response is offered
5. **Human Backup**: If needed, connects to a live mediator
